# Guardian Angel — Market & Business Model (judge sheet)

## One-liner
Free to survive, paid to coordinate — a phone-only seizure safety net that
monetizes family peace of mind and clinic workflow, on hardware users own.

## Market
- ~10M+ Nigerians live with epilepsy; families already spend on drugs,
  transport, and hired help. A ₦-priced safety layer is the cheapest line item.
- 140,000 epilepsy-related deaths/year globally — urgency, not lifestyle.
- Zero hardware cost: any Android phone is the device. Offline-first serves
  low-connectivity areas competitors abandon (wearables + always-online apps).
- Trust moat: NDPR residency (europe-west1), five ethics gates, disclosed
  metrics — clinics recommend what they trust.

## Business model (freemium, ethics-gated)
- **Free forever:** detection, 12s cancel, caregiver alerts (2 caregivers),
  first-aid, GPS, history, meds reminders. Survival is never paywalled.
- **Plus (₦/month or ₦/year, Paystack/Flutterwave):** unlimited caregivers,
  cloud backup + multi-device sync, family/adherence reports, voice minutes,
  clinic sharing.
- **Clinic plan (B2B2C):** dashboard seats, verified e-prescriptions,
  compliance reports per facility. Clinics prescribe the app; families upgrade.

## Go-to-market
1. Pilot with partner clinics (5–10 pairs) → field precision, cancel rate,
   ack latency become the sales deck.
2. Clinics as channel + trust anchor → family acquisition at ~zero CAC.
3. Plus conversion on worry (multi-caregiver households first).
4. Clinic seats scale revenue per facility, not per patient.
